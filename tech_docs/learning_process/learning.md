# Learning Process

## 1. How to Learn Faster with the Feynman Technique

**Question 1: What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.**

The Feynman Technique is a way of learning by explaining a concept in
simple words, identifying what you do not understand, and reviewing it
until you can explain it clearly.

## 3. Learning How to Learn -- TED Talk by Barbara Oakley

**Question 2: In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea
for you?**

The idea I found most interesting was that learning does not happen only
when we are actively studying. Sometimes, taking a short break or doing
something different gives the brain time to process what we have
learned. It reminded me that struggling with a topic does not always
mean I am incapable of understanding it. I may need to change my
approach, take a break, and return to it with a fresh mind.

**Question 3: What are active and diffused modes of thinking?**

-   **Active (focused) mode:** A state of concentration in which we
    direct our attention towards a specific problem or task.
-   **Diffused mode:** A relaxed state of thinking in which the mind can
    make broader connections and approach a problem from a different
    perspective.

Both modes are useful. Focused thinking helps us work through details,
while diffused thinking can help us see connections and find new
approaches.

## 4. Learn Anything in 20 Hours

**Question 4: According to the video, what are the steps to take when
approaching a new topic? Only mention the points.**

1.  Choose a skill you want to learn.
2.  Focus on one skill at a time.
3.  Define your target performance level.
4.  Break the skill down into smaller parts.
5.  Get the tools you need to practise.
6.  Remove distractions and barriers to practice.
7.  Set aside dedicated time for learning.
8.  Create a way to get quick feedback.
9.  Practise in short, focused sessions.
10. Focus on practising enough to improve.

## Learning Principles in the Bootcamp

### Doing things with 100% involvement

Programming requires sustained attention. I need to practise protecting
my attention instead of expecting myself to concentrate perfectly from
the beginning.

Some actions I can take:

-   Keep my phone on silent and away from my workspace while studying.
-   Turn off unnecessary notifications.
-   Avoid social media and other distracting websites during study
    sessions.
-   Decide what I want to finish before starting a session.
-   Track how I spend my study time and review my progress.
-   Take short breaks instead of switching between work and unrelated
    activities.

### How to learn software concepts

When I start learning a new software concept, I will use these questions
as a guide:

1.  Where is this concept used in real applications?
2.  What does it mean, and can I explain it in my own words?
3.  Can I implement it in code or use it in a design?
4.  What practice task or project will help me understand it, and when
    will I complete it?

I should focus on understanding instead of simply finishing tutorials.
Writing notes in my own words, experimenting with code, and explaining
concepts aloud will help me check whether I have actually understood
them.

## Question 5: What are some of the actions you can take going forward to improve your learning process?

- Set a clear goal before starting a new topic.
- Break difficult topics into smaller parts.
- Spend more time practising than watching tutorials.
- Explain concepts in my own words.
- Avoid distractions during study sessions.
- Practise coding regularly.
- Review my mistakes and learn from them.
- Build small projects to apply what I learn.
- Review my progress at the end of each day.
